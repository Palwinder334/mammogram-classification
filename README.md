# mammogram-classification
## Dataset Information and Availability

This research utilizes three mammographic image datasets to evaluate the performance, robustness, and generalizability of the proposed breast cancer image classification models.

**Dataset 1: Hospital-Based Mammography Dataset**

The first dataset consists of mammographic images obtained from the Radiology Department of the AIIMS, Bathinda hospital and used for research purposes with the necessary institutional authorization. Due to data privacy, confidentiality, and institutional data-sharing restrictions, the images are not publicly distributed with this repository. Access may be subject to institutional approval and applicable data-sharing policies.

**Dataset 2: Publicly Available Mammography Dataset**

The second dataset was obtained from a publicly accessible research repository. The original source, publication reference, and access link are provided below.

- Dataset source: Dataset of Breast mammography images with Masses
- Original repository: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ywsbh3ndr8/2
- Publication/reference: Huang ML, Lin TY (2020) Dataset of breast mammography images with masses. Data Brief 31:. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2020.105928
- License/access conditions: Distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license. Users are permitted to access, use, and redistribute the dataset with appropriate attribution to the original authors.

The Python implementation, preprocessing procedures, model architectures, training scripts, and evaluation code are provided in this repository. Researchers can obtain the publicly available datasets from their respective original sources, subject to the specified access conditions, and arrange them according to the directory structure described in the project documentation.
